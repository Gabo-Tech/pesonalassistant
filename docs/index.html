Android · on-device

Assistant

A local voice assistant for notes, calendar, and reminders. Speech and reasoning run on the phone. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

Download Android APK

Version 1.0.0 · sideload · Android 8 and up

  • Install. Open the APK on the phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source — that is expected for a sideload.
  • Models. In Settings, download speech and the language model (or import GGUF files you already have). Typed commands work before that.
  • Privacy. Wake word, notes, and calendar stay on the device. WhatsApp, Signal, and X only open a draft after you tap Send or say send.
